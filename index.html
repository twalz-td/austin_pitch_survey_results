Finding 01

Austin owns food and comedy. Music is contested. Outdoors is borrowed.

Travelers were asked: for each type of experience, which cities would you consider? Austin went head-to-head against Nashville, Denver, Phoenix, and Charlotte. The results define exactly where Austin's pull is real, where it competes, and where investment in paid media would be misplaced.

Austin Nashville Denver Phoenix Charlotte

How Austin reads as a brand

When asked to describe Austin, travelers use identity words, not landmarks. A city defined by feeling travels across markets in a way that attraction-based destinations cannot.

Strategic synthesis

Invest where Austin's gravity already exists.

The survey, the SMARInsights effectiveness data, and the visitor economics point toward the same conclusion. Austin's cultural gravity is real, measurable, and undermonetized. The audiences most responsive to it are also the most economically valuable per trip.

Lead Austin owns it
Culinary and comedy, millennial parents, milestone travelers

Austin wins culinary and comedy consideration outright. Milestone travelers spend $2,024 per trip with a 36% luxury hotel rate. Comedy with 45 to 54 year old visitors who have children averages $3,118, the highest segment in the data. Food festivals (42%) and casual dining (40%) out-pull major concerts as trip motivators.

Support powerful, contested
Music, families with older kids, nostalgia positioning

Music consideration peaks at 45 to 54 year olds at 53%, consistent with a nostalgia and introduction angle. Families traveling with children spend $1,836 vs $1,428 without kids, and 85% of 34 to 44 year old visitors are traveling with children. Music supports the identity case. Nashville wins the head-to-head for a music-specific trip decision.

Amplify on arrival
Outdoors, sports, Gen Z

Denver leads outdoor consideration at 36% vs Austin's 27%. Sport event travelers carry the highest Airbnb rate (40%) and lowest hotel rate (44%) in the study, generating below-average HOT despite strong total spend. Gen Z accounts for 54% of ad-aware households but only 20% of actual visitors. Outdoors and sports work as in-destination amplifiers once Austin has been chosen for another reason.

Focused investment

Target milestone travelers and cultural event visitors in fly markets. These two segments carry the highest hotel rates (64 to 76%), highest luxury rates (33 to 36%), and above-average spend per trip. Culinary-led creative in East Coast and Midwest fly markets. Household income $100k and above with children in the home.

Fly market ROI: $874:$1 · Milestone HOT per trip: $107 · Cultural event hotel rate: 76%

Expanded investment

Layer in multi-pillar creative that communicates the combination of food, music, comedy, and outdoor in one trip. Multi-pillar visitors spend $2,203 and $607 on transport, indicating they are flying from farther markets. Add West Coast markets where entertainment consideration is highest. Test comedy-led creative for 25 to 44 year old households.

Multi-pillar spend: $2,203 · Multi-pillar transport: $607 · Comedy consideration 25 to 34: 50%

The through-line
Association leads familiarity leads intent leads visit leads belief.
MMGY brand survey surfaces Austin's cultural associations. SMARInsights proves ads convert association into familiarity and intent. The visit itself closes the pillar consideration gap by 19 to 27 points. Three independent data sources, one consistent direction.
